the ordinary path through `pci_disable_device()` and
`pci_set_power_state(pdev, 3)`; nonzero skips those calls and goes straight to

consistent with the nonzero branch. Thus an explicit D3hot setter exists in
the binary but is not part of that observed connected-DRV path. Physical PCI
state during sleep remains unknown.
